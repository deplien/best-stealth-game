## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (or serve the folder with `node serve.ts`) — no build step, no network.

- **Move** — WASD or the arrow keys
- **Sneak** — hold Shift: slower and quieter, and the guards' flashlights fill with suspicion far more slowly
- **Throw a coin** — click anywhere: the ping draws a guard away from his patrol to investigate
- **Pause** — P

Steal the Star Diamond from its pedestal in each of the three halls, then reach the green EXIT door. A guard's beam turns orange when he suspects something and red when he is sure; break line of sight behind the display cases and suspicion drains away again. Fill the detection meter and the night is over.
